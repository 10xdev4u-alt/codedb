# Research audit — 2026-10-23

The `/tmp` wipe removed the working copies and the transcript's shell history, so the
research had to be re-checked rather than inherited. This audit re-reads every
source-backed claim in `EMBEDDINGGEMMA2_REPORT.md`, re-queries every cited link, and
recomputes the GitHub sweep from its manifests.

## Verdict

The sweep is exact. The report is directionally right and now factually corrected.
Three claims were wrong in a way that could mislead a build decision, eight numbers
had drifted, two links were dead or renamed, and two "evidence" files contained
nothing. All of that is fixed in place, and the checks are now scripts instead of
trust.

Nothing found here invalidates the case for putting EmbeddingGemma 2 behind codedb.
